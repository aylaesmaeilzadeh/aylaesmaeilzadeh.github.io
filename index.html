Ayla Esmaeilzadeh

Computational Biologist | M.Sc. in Biotechnology

I am passionate about understanding biological systems at the molecular level through computational approaches. My educational background is in biotechnology and cell and molecular biology, and my research focuses on molecular dynamics simulations and structural bioinformatics to investigate biomolecular and nanomaterial interactions, drug delivery systems, lipid membranes, and the structure and function of proteins.

My broader research interests include uncovering the molecular mechanisms underlying biological processes and diseases, particularly cancer, neurodegenerative diseases, and women's health. In the long term, I aim to integrate molecular modeling, molecular simulations, and machine learning with experimental approaches to investigate complex biological questions, advance our understanding of disease mechanisms, and support the rational design of drugs and therapeutic strategies.
